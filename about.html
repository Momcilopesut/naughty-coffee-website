(Roastery)

One room.
From green bean
to last sip.

Sourcing, roasting and brewing all happen under one roof. We follow every coffee the whole way, so nothing gets lost between the farm and your cup.

The process in 3 phases
(Phase_01)

Sourcing

We cup samples from importers who work directly with producers, and choose small lots where altitude, soil and care give the coffee a clear character. We pay well above commodity price for them.

(Phase_02)

Roasting

Small 12kg batches with a profile for every coffee, logged and adjusted each week. We roast light enough to taste where a coffee came from, then rest it so its flavours settle.

(Phase_03)

Brewing

Our baristas taste every coffee each morning and set the recipes for the bar: dose, grind, time and temperature, written down and checked again at noon.

Story (Name)

Why “Kum”?

In the Balkans, your kum is the person you trust most — the one who stands beside you at your wedding and christens your children. That's what we want to be for your mornings: the place you come back to, where the coffee is always good and someone remembers your order.

Story(Milestones)
  1. Year one

    A kitchen with a roaster in it

    We open as a breakfast spot, roasting two coffees on a five-kilo machine in the corner.

  2. Year two

    Beans go online

    Regulars kept asking to take coffee home, so we started shipping it.

  3. Year three

    A bigger drum

    A 12kg roaster replaces the original. The old one now holds plants on the retail shelf.

  4. Today

    Six origins, one room

    Coffee bar, roastery, kitchen and a shelf of tees and hats, all under one roof.
