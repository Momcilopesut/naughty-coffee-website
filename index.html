Concept (001)

The coffee bar

Espresso, filter and pour over from a rotating shelf of single origins. Every recipe is tasted and dialled in each morning, so the cup you get at four is as good as the one at eight.

Concept (002)

The roastery

Twelve kilos at a time, a few metres from the counter. Each coffee gets its own roast profile, built over weeks of test batches and blind cupping, and rested before it reaches your cup.

Concept (003)

The kitchen / breakfast, properly

A short menu cooked from scratch every morning: soft eggs, house sourdough, miso butter, crispy rice. Simple food, made carefully, served until mid-afternoon.
